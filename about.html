About Reliable Land Survey Consultancy

A professional surveying and geospatial service provider focused on accurate field data, engineering-ready deliverables and dependable site support for infrastructure projects.

ENGINEERING POSITIONING

Specialized Geospatial Solutions in Pune

Reliable Land Survey Consultancy provides specialized surveying and geospatial solutions for Metro Rail, Railway, Highway, Road and Infrastructure Projects, enhanced with modern drone, RTK, photogrammetry and LiDAR-based mapping workflows.

Operating from Pune, Maharashtra, under the leadership of proprietor MAHESH DEHMUKH, our engineering consultancy bridges the gap between field survey physics and computational civil design. We deliver high-fidelity spatial data that project directors, design consultants, and EPC contractors can rely on without uncertainty.
