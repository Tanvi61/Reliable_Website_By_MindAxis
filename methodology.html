Survey Methodology & QA/QC

A disciplined, 6-phase engineering process designed to eliminate errors, guarantee baseline closure, and provide verifiable spatial data.

SIX-STAGE SURVEY EXECUTION

The Engineering Survey Lifecycle

From initial satellite reconnaissance to field validation and final sign-off, every stage is audited for precision.

01
PHASE 01

Project Study & Reconnaissance

Initial site inspection, existing Survey of India (SOI) benchmark recovery, corridor right-of-way assessment, terrain hazard identification, and drone flight corridor airspace clearance planning.

Key Outputs: Reconnaissance Survey Report, Benchmark Recovery Sheets, Mobilization Schedule
02
PHASE 02

Control Establishment

Setting up robust geodetic primary control points using dual-frequency DGPS/GNSS static observations. Monumentation of durable concrete pillars for Temporary Bench Marks (TBM) tied to national grid datums (WGS84 / UTM).

Key Outputs: DGPS Baseline Network Adjustment, TBM Elevation Schedules, Monument Coordinate Cards
03
PHASE 03

Detailed Field Observations

High-accuracy Total Station traverse observations, digital auto level staff readings, RTK drone photogrammetry flights, and active LiDAR laser scanning passes. Captures natural terrain relief and all civil structures.

Key Outputs: Raw Electronic Observation Logs, Field Level Books, Aerial Flight Logs & Raw LiDAR Scans
04
PHASE 04

Data Processing & Drafting

Computation of coordinate geometries, angular and linear closure error adjustments, LiDAR point cloud classification (ground vs. non-ground), aerial orthomosaic stitching, and CAD drafting compliant with civil codes.

Key Outputs: Layered AutoCAD DWG Drawings, Classified Point Cloud (.LAS), GeoTIFF Rasters, L-Sections
05
PHASE 05

Independent Checking

Secondary audit team conducts independent spot checks in the field, drawing-to-field alignment cross-checks, benchmark level reruns, and coordinate verification before draft approval.

Key Outputs: Independent Quality Audit Certificate, Verification Delta Tables, Review Comments Log
06
PHASE 06

Final Deliverables & Report

Compiling approved topographical plans, profiles, cross-sections, GIS shapefiles, DEM/DTMs, digital field raw data, and comprehensive survey engineering documentation signed by technical supervisors.

Key Outputs: Final Bound Technical Survey Report, Digital CAD / GIS Dataset Media, Certification
QUALITY ASSURANCE & QUALITY CONTROL

The 7 Pillars of Survey Quality

Fundamental principles ensuring that every coordinate and elevation delivered is indisputable.

✓ Instrument Readiness Checks

Daily verification of instrument calibration, optical plummet centering, tribrach levelling, electronic tilt-sensors, and battery capacities prior to field departure.

✓ Reliable Control Points

Establishing deep-ground monumented benchmarks with redundant GNSS baselines, preventing baseline drift across extended linear infrastructure corridors.

✓ Field Verification

Immediate on-site cross-checking of critical features, intersection angles, and physical boundaries by experienced survey supervisors before leaving the site.

✓ Coordinate / Level Cross-Checks

Closed loop traverse computations and double-run differential leveling loops with strict allowable closure error formulas applied.

✓ Drawing-to-Field Validation

Draft CAD drawings are printed and taken back to the project site for visual ground verification against existing physical ground features.

✓ Documented Survey Records

Maintaining meticulous electronic raw data logs, job books, climate observations, and digital photos of all recovered survey monuments.

✓ Technical Review Before Submission

Final scrutiny and sign-off by senior site engineers and consultancy leadership prior to client or tender submission.
